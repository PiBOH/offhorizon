3D artists

We're looking for3D artists.

OffHorizon welcomes 3D artists to make cosmetics for OffHorizon players: things players wear and carry that other OffHorizon players see in every world and on every server. If you like making small, blocky models, we'd like to hear from you.

What we'd make together

  • Hats and other head pieces.
  • Back items, such as backpacks or rigid wings.
  • Small pets that ride on a player's shoulder, or companions that float beside them.
  • Auras and trails made of a few particles.
  • Emotes: short animations for the player's own model.

Cosmetics only change how a player looks, never how the game plays.

What fits

  • Low-poly models in Minecraft's blocky style, built from a few dozen cubes, with small pixel textures (16 to 64 pixels).
  • Your own designs. No copies of Minecraft's mobs and items, or of anyone else's work.
  • Art for every age, that still reads at a distance.
  • No capes, or anything that hangs from the shoulders like one.
  • Blockbench is welcome. Animation is a plus, not a requirement.

Get in touch

Join OffHorizon's Discord and say you're a 3D artist, with a few pieces you've made: links or pictures are fine. If you'd rather write privately, write through lu4ovic.com (opens in a new tab).

Privacy

This site sets no cookies, has no analytics or trackers and loads nothing from third parties except GitHub. When you open a release, the server keeps a one-way hash of your IP address so that release counts each visitor once; the address itself is never stored. To show the latest releases, patch notes, links and contributors, your browser asks api.github.com and raw.githubusercontent.com for them, and avatars.githubusercontent.com for the contributors' pictures; GitHub's privacy statement (opens in a new tab) covers those requests. The launcher sends nothing unless you allow it: what it can send, and how to delete it (opens in a new tab).
