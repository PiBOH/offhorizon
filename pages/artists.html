3D artists

The art behind the world

Horizon's blocks, mobs, items and their textures are modelled and textured by 3D artists. OffHorizon ships the same art, unchanged.

What the artists make

  • Block and item models, and the textures that go with them.
  • Mobs, their animations and their poses.
  • The props the game shows in menus and in the world.

How credit works

Every asset keeps the credit its author gave it. The project's notices file lists the third-party assets and their licences, and each asset in the repository carries its author in the history. OffHorizon adds no art of its own and removes no credit.

Reporting a missing credit

If an asset you made is here without the credit you want, open an issue on the tracker with the file and the credit line to use; it is fixed in the next build.

Making art for the project

Artwork follows the vanilla look: the same proportions, the same palette, the same pixel density. Contributions go through the Horizon project, which is where the art is made, and reach OffHorizon with the next upstream sync.
